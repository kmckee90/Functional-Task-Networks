Root /base/ seen-atom logit regularizer

    loss = task_loss + output_entropy_term + lambda * mean_i(logit_i * seen_i)

seen_i is1 if mask position i was active in ANY archived training mask, else0.
Each position counts once. The mean divides by ALL mask positions, not the
number of seen atoms. Raw mask logits are used without sigmoid, clipping or
conversion to hard masks. Positive lambda pushes seen logits downward; the
term may become negative. Existing model-specific logit constraints remain.

RECONFIG_OVERLAP_LAMBDA is the coefficient; Default0 disables it.
RECONFIG_OVERLAP_MODE is now "seen_logits". Legacy "seen_mass", "bump", and
"gated" selectors are accepted but all use the new mean-logit formula.
Threshold and temperature fields are unused. User coefficients are preserved.

Candidate scoring uses the current search logits, including when comparing
stored hard masks (which have no archived logits). In staged searches, frozen
mask-only positions without logits contribute zero; active positions use raw
logits. Recurrent mask transitions use their detached-weight raw scores.

Both replay and no-replay retain distinct training masks after weight updates.
Replay-buffer eviction does not remove archive entries. Evaluation/search never
adds masks; root evaluation uses the regularizer too. The archive persists in
state_dict and survives FTN expansion. Old checkpoints without an archive start
empty. Custom loops must call record_training_mask() after weight updates.

Only /base/ changed; projects/bayesian_adv remains unchanged.
