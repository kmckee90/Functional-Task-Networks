Training mask overlap regularization

Config overrides (available to basic FTN, LoRAFTN, BayesFTN and all shared
BayesMaskReconfig backbones, including the IC experiment configs):

    RECONFIG_OVERLAP_MODE = "bump"     # or "gated"
    RECONFIG_OVERLAP_LAMBDA = 0.0      # disabled by default
    RECONFIG_OVERLAP_THRESHOLD = 0.5
    RECONFIG_OVERLAP_TEMPERATURE = 0.2

Direct constructors accept overlap_mode, overlap_lambda, overlap_threshold,
overlap_temperature. Existing config parameter bundles include these options.

o = sum(candidate * reference) / sum(reference)
bump:  lambda * o * (1-o)
gated: lambda * o * sigmoid((threshold-o)/temperature)

The reference is self.m at entry to each training search, detached and cloned
once. A zero/uninitialized mask skips the penalty. The reference never follows
optimizer steps. Ordinary and changepoint searches both use it; the next call
takes a new snapshot. No implicit archive or task labels are introduced.
Recurrent masks normalize by total activity across blocks, not per-block K.

Hard searches use the existing straight-through gradient; soft/fast searches
use their sigmoid masks. Lambda zero bypasses objective modification and does
not change random-number consumption. Backbone weights remain detached.
Candidate selection uses task loss + overlap cost. Bayes optimization retains
its additional existing KL term; its take-best ranking historically excludes
KL and continues to do so. Bayes last_reconfig_stats includes overlap_penalty.

Cold evaluation is unchanged and uses no reference penalty, preserving its
independence from the last training task. Warm training searches do use it.
No aggregation across archived masks is implemented in this first version.

The gated penalty approaches zero at high overlap but is not exactly zero at
o=1 for finite T. Threshold is the sigmoid midpoint, not the exact derivative
sign-change point. With broad T the transition and residual are intentional.
These penalties influence selection, not protection of shared weights during
subsequent training. They do not establish that a changepoint is false.
