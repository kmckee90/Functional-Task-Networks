Current progress towards AGI
    "Agentic" in 2026 refers almost exclusively to LLM agents. Language itself is a limited, pragmatic model of human experience. That makes LLM agents simulations of a simulation.
    While LLMs are useful, real human understanding, perceptions, and motivations cannot be reconstructed just from how they are communicated.
    It is also not guaranteed how much LLM agents can produce novel insights, lacking the experiential sources of their own data.

Naturalistic AGI: Exploration with Continual Learning
    - HYPOTHESES: 
        0 Continual learning and exploration, wired together, will result in continual, autonomous discovery of knowledge and skills in open-ended environments, even without any reward functions.

        1 Perpetual and intelligent knowledge-seeking reflects the interplay of several distinct novelty metrics: 
            - (a) observation familiarity (b) observation reconstructability (c) predictability (d) forecast variance -- Other? 
            For example, even a simple linkage between two novelty metrics leads to interesting predictions:
            - An exploration strategy seeks experiences that diversify data and challenge the model.
            - The model learns to forecast the outcomes of the exploration policy, which is constantly drifting.
            - The exploration policy thus continually adapts to maximizes novelty over its own behaviors in addition to exploiting regularities in the environment that lead to novelty.
            - The original memory density objective continues to penalize long repetition of the same observations, exhausting trivial outcomes. 
            As a result, the agent's exploration capitalizes on environmental regularities but itself is pressured to avoid becoming too systematic and repetitious.

            This is only a simple sketch of the reasoning I will apply. The reality is that the interplay between objectives should be highly emergent.
            The project therefore requires defining behavioral outcomes and iterating on the agent's design until they emerge.

        2 Conscious behavior reflects a sensitivity to recursive self-predictability.
            Meta-cognition is not a fixed hierarchical structure, but a flexible, continual ability to observe one's behavior, one's internal reactions to that behavior, one's reactions to those reactions, etc.
            I propose that behavioral self-adaptation akin to conscious awareness can be simulated by finding the right recursive interplay of novelty pressures.     

        3 Different novelty signals play different roles depending on developmental stage.
           - Memory density comes first: model-free, very fast, rapidly provides data diversity, bodily/environmental exploration, "normalized" behavior ranges, and most basic skills.
           - Reconstruction error comes early too: Basic genearlized "seeing" ability of the model, without requiring predictive knowledge.
           - Prediction error, forecast variance: Later stage, dependent on highly general reconstruction ability and diversified data.

    - IMPORTANCE
        A successful prototype of naturalistic, autonomous AGI will:
        - Provide a fundamental framework for hands-off, self-improving agency, while allowing domain specializations. 
        - Find novel solutions hard problems based on its own exploration and reasoning.
        - Eliminate much of the brittle, hand-engineered aspects of agents and autonomous robotics

        - Proof of concept for emergent and expected capabilities: skill and knowledge pursuit, internal resource management, self-awareness.
        - Scientific understanding and simulation of agency and cognitive evolution in living creatures, of human curiosity.
