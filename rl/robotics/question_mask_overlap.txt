================================================================================
WHAT GETS REPLAYED VIA THE MASK-OVERLAP QUERY?
  Investigation of train.py (DDPG) and train_grid_dqn_async.py (DQN)
================================================================================

SHORT ANSWER
------------
Yes — the FTN component DOES do mask-overlap-filtered replay in both files.

The unit that gets replayed is the INDIVIDUAL TRANSITION TUPLE
(s, a, r, s', done), sampled uniformly at random.

It is NOT episodes, NOT complete episodes, and NOT "complete episodes where
reward was observed." It is a flat pool of single transitions, drawn from the
ENTIRE task phase — including failed exploration episodes — with episode
boundaries discarded (they survive only implicitly as the `done` column).

So of your multiple-choice options, the answer is:
  -> "batches of tuples but not necessarily complete episodes"
and additionally, the pool those tuples come from is everything collected
during the task (exploration successes AND failures AND all exploitation
episodes), not a reward-filtered subset.


WHERE THE MECHANISM LIVES
-------------------------
DDPG (train.py):
  - Buffer:        MaskedReplayBufferDDPG          (lines 373-488)
  - Query:         sample_filtered(...)            (lines 455-488)
  - Archive:       archive_phase(m_a, m_c)         (lines 438-453)
  - Called from:   main loop                       (lines 1020-1036, 1071)

DQN async (train_grid_dqn_async.py):
  - Buffer:        MaskedReplayBufferDQN           (lines 283-399)
  - Query:         sample_filtered(...)            (lines 371-399)
  - Archive:       archive_phase(m)                (lines 356-369)
  - Called from:   main loop                       (lines 953-967, 1006)

The two are structurally identical; DDPG carries two masks per phase
(actor m_a, critic m_c), DQN carries one (m).


HOW DATA IS STORED, STEP BY STEP
--------------------------------
1. At the start of each task phase, the current-phase ring buffer is wiped:
      replay.clear_current()
   (train.py:946 / async:900). This only resets ptr/size; the underlying
   arrays are reused.

2. EVERY episode collected during the phase is flattened transition-by-
   transition into that ring buffer via add_episode():
     - All EXPLORATION episodes — successful AND unsuccessful — are pushed
       (train.py:997-998 / async:934-936).
     - All K EXPLOITATION episodes are pushed, regardless of outcome
       (train.py:1008-1009 / async:945-947).
   _collect_episode / _collect_episode_dqn store every step of the rollout;
   the per-episode `success` flag is recorded but is NOT used to gate storage
   (train.py:687-711 / async:580-609). There is no reward/success filtering of
   what enters the buffer.

3. add_episode() does a bulk copy of the episode's (obs, act, rew, next_obs,
   done) arrays into the ring buffer (train.py:392-422 / async:309-340). Once
   in the buffer, transitions are an undifferentiated flat pool — episode
   identity is gone. The only residue of episode structure is the `done`
   column (1.0 on the last step of each episode).

4. At phase end, archive_phase() snapshots the WHOLE current ring buffer
   (self.obs[:size], etc.) as a single "past phase" record, tagged with the
   mask(s) that were active AT ARCHIVE TIME — i.e. the final mask of the phase
   (train.py:438-453 / async:356-369). A stacked tensor of all past-phase
   masks is cached for vectorized overlap.


HOW THE QUERY SELECTS WHAT TO REPLAY
------------------------------------
During exploitation, after each ordinary DDPG/DQN update, the code does
mask_replay_n_per_step extra updates on past data (train.py:1024-1036 /
async:957-967). Each call to sample_filtered:

  a) Computes overlap rho between the CURRENT mask and each archived phase's
     mask:   rho = (m_past * m_cur).sum() / m_cur.sum()
     (fraction of the current active neurons that the past phase also used).
     DDPG computes this for both actor and critic.

  b) Keeps a phase as a candidate iff rho in (rho_low, rho_high]
     (defaults 0.2, 0.8). DDPG keeps a phase if EITHER actor OR critic mask
     overlaps in-range (train.py:469-471 / async:382).

  c) Picks ONE qualifying phase at random, then samples `n` transitions
     UNIFORMLY AT RANDOM from that phase's entire transition pool
     (train.py:476-487 / async:387-398).

  d) Returns (batch, m_old [+ m_c_old]) so the replay gradient step is run
     under the OLD phase's mask, not the current one
     (train.py:1036 / async:967).

So a single replay step = n random transitions from one partially-overlapping
past task, evaluated under that task's stored mask.


CAVEATS / THINGS YOU MAY OR MAY NOT HAVE INTENDED
-------------------------------------------------
* MASK/DATA MISMATCH WITHIN A PHASE. archive_phase tags the buffer with the
  FINAL phase mask only. But the buffer also holds transitions collected under
  earlier masks: the exploration episodes (collected under the prior/exploration
  mask, before the boundary reconfig at train.py:983) and any transitions from
  before each mid-phase reconfig (train.py:1052 / async:991, reconfig_every_trials).
  All of these are later replayed as if collected under the single final mask.
  If reconfig moves the mask much during a phase, the stored (transitions, mask)
  pairing is approximate.

* NO REWARD FILTERING. Failed exploration rollouts (which can be the large
  majority — exploration runs until only n_successful_explore rewards) are
  stored and replayed with equal weight to successful ones. If you wanted the
  cross-task replay to consolidate *competent* behavior, it currently does not;
  it replays the whole behavioral mixture of the past task.

* RING-BUFFER TRUNCATION. The archive is a snapshot of the ring buffer, capped
  at replay_capacity. If a phase's total steps exceed capacity, the earliest
  transitions are overwritten before archiving, so the archive holds only the
  most recent <=capacity transitions of that phase. DDPG capacity is 200k
  (unlikely to bite); the DQN grid episodes are short, also unlikely. Worth
  knowing it's "last N transitions of the phase," not "all of them," in the
  limit.

* `done` INCLUDES TRUNCATION. d_buf stores float(term or trunc)
  (async:595; same pattern in train.py). Truncated-but-not-terminal final steps
  are marked done=1, which zeros the bootstrap term in the TD target for those
  transitions. Standard DDPG/DQN subtlety, independent of the replay question,
  but it rides along in every replayed batch.
